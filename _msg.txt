model: v12 -- hair colour fixed, hair staggered and thinned; horns and arms still hidden

Six fixes requested; four landed, two did not.

Hair colour was the real complaint and it is fixed. The old build gave every long board its
own gradient UV, so each segment ran light-to-dark independently and the hair shimmered in
stripes. Now every cube has exactly one flat colour chosen by its vertical position in the
strand, so the gradient runs once across the whole head of hair. Palette sampled from the
official collab artwork (ref_015) rather than guessed: root #7a8ab0, upper #8697be, middle
#9ea7d4, lower #9aa4d6, tips #a8b0dc, plus #cfd6f2 highlight and #6f7ea6 inner shadow,
seven steps painted as flat blocks in both 1024 textures with all hair UVs pointing at them.

Back hair went from three equal-length chains to four groups of length 15.0 / 19.0 / 17.0 /
13.5 with 12 / 10 / 11 / 12 segments, offset 0.1-3.4 in x and 0.05-0.25 in z, so the back
now shows staggered strand ends instead of a wall. Side hair halved from 58 and 55 cubes to
3 strands of 10 segments each per side, lengths 20.5 / 18.5 / 22.0, uniform 0.58-0.66
thickness, pulled back 0.85 off the cheek. Neck strip shortened from 3.0 to 1.1 and switched
to skin tone. Total 587 to 466 cubes.

Failed: the horns were rebuilt as nine segments tapering 2.5 to 1.0 along a 260-degree curl
but the hair mass completely buries them, so only two nubs show above the crown. The arms
moved from +/-3.23 to +/-3.5 while the torso narrowed 0.86, yet the side hair still covers
the upper arm in front view.

Comparing against the official artwork afterwards found the remaining drift: the reference
hair is far more voluminous and fluffy than ours, the horns are large dark-blue curled horns
occupying about half the head height, the eyes are larger and brighter, the chest bow and
brooch are bigger, and the skirt has more lace layering plus a long train.
