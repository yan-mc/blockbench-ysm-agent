feat: isolate zoom, corrected proportions, hair and eye corrections (v0.9.1)

Part review: isolate now accepts zoom (default 1, useful range 1.5-3). withIsolation
computes the bounding box of the kept cubes and stores it, modelBounds returns that
override while isolated, so the framing tightens to the isolated part and zoom scales it
further. Exposed on bb_look and bb_render_views alongside isolate, so a part can be
inspected up close from several angles in one call.

Proportions corrected. For three rounds I chased a head-to-body target of 4.87, which was
a mis-measurement: it came from a head height that included hair. Measuring bone pivots
and cube bounds across 140 models gives real medians -- total height / head height 7.31
(p25 6.54 / p75 9.88), shoulder width / head width 1.01, hip width / head width 0.69,
torso length / head height 1.77, arm length / head height 1.49, head 7x7x7. The corpus is
a realistic seven-head build, not chibi. Applied to our model this exposes the actual
fault: total/head is 7.50, close to target, while shoulder width / head width is 1.52
against 1.01 -- the shoulders are half again too wide, which is what reads as stocky.

Hair: measured 714 strands of four or more cubes. Within a single strand the coefficient
of variation of thickness is a median 0.11 (p25 0.00) and 68% fall below 0.3, with a median
of 10 cubes per strand. So strands are uniform-thickness chains, not tapered wedges; the
curve comes from many slightly rotated cubes in series, which is also what keeps the top of
the head from reading as a hard corner. The taper that 61% of models do show is only the
last one or two cubes.

Eyes: the lid plate measures 2.00 wide by 1.52 tall, i.e. 1.32:1 and clearly wider than
tall. Anime eyes are flat, and every horizontal dimension should exceed its vertical
counterpart.
