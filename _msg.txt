fix+docs: isolation actually hides geometry; eye doctrine; part-level inspection

The isolate feature framed correctly but still rendered the hidden parts: setting
Group.visibility / Cube.visibility in Blockbench does not propagate to the three.js mesh,
so the renderer kept drawing everything. withIsolation now writes mesh.visible directly
and restores it in the finally block. Verified on the live model: isolating AllHead now
returns just the head and hair, auto-framed, and nothing else.

Skill additions:
- 3.1.4 eye doctrine from the 157-model eye survey. Single-eye geometry is a median of 17
  cubes but p25 of 8, and the best examples use one or two flat plates (nagato: one
  3x3.4 block plus a highlight block; alice: a single 2x2 lid plate; ump45: a 2x2 lid plus
  a narrower 1.4x2 iris plate). The largest block has a median width-to-height ratio of
  1.02, i.e. square -- round is painted, not built. Three causes of dead-fish eyes are all
  painting faults: a perfectly round centred iris, no upper lash line or too faint a one,
  and perfect symmetry with a centred highlight. Six painting rules follow: thick 1-2
  texel upper lash angled 15-25 degrees up at the outer corner, iris clipped 20-30% by the
  lid and slightly taller than wide, pupil high, one or two highlights off to the upper
  left, lower lid just a bright line, eye white only at the bottom and sides.
- 3.1.5 part-level inspection: do not render the whole model for every check, use
  isolate with one bone or a comma-separated list, which hides the rest and auto-frames.
